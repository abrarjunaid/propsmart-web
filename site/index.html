Questions brokerage owners ask

Before you book.

Is it only for Dubai brokerages?

It was built in Dubai for Dubai: Bayut and Property Finder integrations, AED, cheque-based rentals, off-plan pipelines, co-agent splits. Brokerages elsewhere in the GCC run on it too; the portal connectors are the only Dubai-specific piece, and we add new ones on request.

How long does onboarding take?

A configured workspace is typically ready within a week of the demo. Agents get access with their leads already loaded, and most are working from it on day one because the app mirrors how they already work in WhatsApp.

Can we migrate from our current CRM or spreadsheets?

Yes. Leads, listings, buildings and owner lists import from Excel. Historical deals and commission can be loaded so your year-to-date numbers are correct from the start.

What does "custom modules in days" actually mean?

If your brokerage needs a workflow the product doesn't have (a driver schedule, a referral-partner ledger, a specific approval chain), we specify it with you and build it. Because the codebase is engineered with AI, a scoped module usually ships within the week, into your workspace, without waiting on a public roadmap.

Where is our data, and can we leave?

Your workspace is yours. Everything — leads, notes, call logs, deals, owner lists — exports to Excel at any time from inside the app. No export fees, no notice period on your data.
